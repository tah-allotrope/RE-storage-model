Final Report · Sprint Synthesis

Two-Component Tariff, Reachable

Vietnam's Decree 146/2025 two-component tariff (Ca energy + Cp demand charge) was a fully-shipped Sprint 4 pipeline feature — completely invisible in the web tool. The form hardcoded "tariff_structure": "1-component" and Cp_demand: 0.0, so demand_charge_savings_usd was always zero and run_tariff_mode_comparison() was never called by any handler. In three phases we threaded tariff_mode through every backend endpoint, added a tariff-mode select + Cp/Ca inputs to the form, and built a dedicated comparison view that headlines the Decree 146/2025 trade-off: lower grid-energy savings, new demand-charge savings. Shipped to main, fully tested.

02 · Background & Objective

Why this mattered now

Decree 146/2025 introduces a two-component retail tariff in Vietnam: an energy charge (Ca, VND/kWh) at lower rates plus a capacity charge (Cp, VND/kW/month) on monthly peak demand. For a solar-plus-battery offtaker, the trade-off is real money: ~$303.9k → ~$203.1k grid-energy savings against ~$8.0k new demand-charge savings (Sprint 4 Emivest reference). The model could quantify this; the web tool could not even ask the question. Without GAP-03, the most recent shipped model feature was completely orphaned from the user-facing surface.

07 · Findings & Recommendation

What we found, what we advise

Findings

  • The plan's assumption that the JSON loader picks up tariff_structure from the payload was wrong — only the Excel loader does that. Required threading tariff_mode as a kwarg through every handler, including the download endpoints (otherwise downloads would silently revert to 1-component after a 2-component run).
  • Validating tariff_mode at the payload-build boundary (resolve_tariff_mode) lets every handler share the same 400-on-bad-input path with zero extra plumbing.
  • Categorical comparison (run-the-pipeline-twice) is qualitatively different from a numeric sweep and gets its own endpoint and UI rather than being squeezed into the existing scenario / sensitivity surfaces.
  • Older API responses lack demand_charge_savings_usd / tariff_mode — making those fields optional on ModelKpis preserves backward compat for free.

Test growth (TDD)

Recommendation: ship as-is. The Sprint 4 model feature is now end-to-end reachable, the comparison view headlines the Decree 146/2025 trade-off, and the 1-component default keeps the form simple for users not on the pilot. Surface voltage-tier warnings (RISK-01-01) only if the silent-fallback shows up in production — most projects will be on 22 kV where the matrix is complete.

09 · Risks / Open Questions

What to watch

  • Silent fallback for non-22 kV voltage tiers: if retail_tariff_matrix.tariff_options lacks a 2-component entry for the requested voltage tier, the loader logs a warning and runs 1-component. The web tool never sees that warning today. Mitigation deferred (Step 4 above); for now, document in activeContext.md and warn pilot users to manually verify.
  • Manual UI verification outstanding: 38 backend tests + frontend build pass, but the dropdown → form-reveal → comparison-table flow has not been exercised against a live backend. Step 1 above is the gate.
  • Default Ca rates are 22 kV specific: 1190 / 1833 / 3398 VND/kWh are the Sprint 4 22 kV reference. If non-22 kV users opt into 2-component without overriding these, the numbers will be wrong by tier. Field labels mention "(VND/kWh)" but not the voltage assumption.
  • Pre-existing mypy noise: the systemic tuple[Response, int] warnings still apply (lessons.md L-006). No new regressions.
  • Not yet live: users benefit only once GAP-04 deploys the app for real.
