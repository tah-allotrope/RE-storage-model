03 / Math / Algorithm Used

Math / Algorithm Used

Core delta math:
Delta Revenue (USD) = (new year1_dppa_revenue + new year1_grid_savings)
                    - (old year1_dppa_revenue + old year1_grid_savings)

Delta Revenue (%)   = Delta Revenue / Old Revenue * 100
Delta IRR (pp)      = (new project_irr - old project_irr) * 100
Delta NPV (USD)     = new npv_usd - old npv_usd

Revenue decomposition:
- Morning peak uplift: aligned rows where PV was peak-priced before but not after.
- BESS cycle reduction: aligned rows where peak-period discharge dropped under the new tariff.
- Off-peak rate changes: difference in off-peak grid-savings totals.
- Shifted peak timing: residual needed for the driver buckets to add back to the observed total delta.

Average-day dispatch:
- Group hourly settlement output by hour-of-day.
- Mean direct PV, BESS discharge, and post-RE grid import across the available year.

06 / Limitations / Second-Best Alternative

Limitations / Second-Best Alternative

  • Ecoplexus could be included in the KPI delta table, but not in the hourly decomposition or average-day plot because run_full_model() does not currently surface _hourly_df.
  • The decomposition is intentionally pragmatic: three directly attributable buckets plus one timing residual so the pieces reconcile to the observed total.
  • The second-best alternative would have been a one-off notebook or markdown-only analysis, but that would not have been reproducible or test-locked in the repository.
