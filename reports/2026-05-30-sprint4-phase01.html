Sprint 4 Phase 01 — Tariff Mode in Pipeline

Added a tariff_mode parameter that switches the settlement engine between Vietnam's single-component EVN energy tariff and the Decree 146/2025 two-component tariff. In two-component mode the pipeline settles energy on the lower Ca rates and activates the capacity charge (Cp) demand-charge savings stream. Default behaviour is unchanged.

03 / Math / Algorithm Used

Math / Algorithm Used

No new solver. The phase reuses the existing demand-charge formula and adds a rate-selection switch:

  • Demand-charge savings = Σmonths [(baseline_peak_kW − post-RE_peak_kW) × Cp] / FX  — already in demand_charge.py, now fed a non-zero Cp.
  • Energy settlement uses ca_tariff_rates (USD/kWh = CaVND / FX) in place of the standard EVN rate dict when tariff_mode == "2-component".
  • Cp gating: Cp is forced to 0 under 1-component because a single energy rate has no capacity component to reduce.

06 / Limitations / Second-Best Alternative

Limitations / Second-Best Alternative

  • In PHASE-01, Ca rates and Cp are passed as explicit arguments. Auto-loading from the project's retail_tariff_matrix is PHASE-02 — until then a caller must supply them.
  • Second-best alternative: a standalone two-component settlement module. Rejected — the math is identical (energy × rate + demand × Cp); only the rate values change, so a mode flag avoids duplicating settlement logic (plan ALT-001).
  • Demand-charge savings depend on the monthly peak-reduction profile, which is modest for this battery dispatch (hence ~$8k, not a headline driver here).
