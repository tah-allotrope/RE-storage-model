What changed

1

Tax marker parsing fixed

Workbook Assumption-sheet tax markers are now interpreted as period endpoints rather than raw durations, which removed one major source of cash-flow drift.

2

Debt sizing switched toward sculpting

The debt module now builds a DSCR-shaped schedule instead of a flat annuity schedule, which moves the model closer to the workbook’s GoalSeek-driven debt pattern.

3

Waterfall timing aligned

MRA is now included in EBITDA timing, signed tax rows flow through the waterfall more like Excel, and FCFE is assembled from CFADS plus principal less interest.

4

Parity behavior pinned

New targeted tests lock the tax-marker conversion and sculpted debt schedule so later parity iterations do not silently regress.
